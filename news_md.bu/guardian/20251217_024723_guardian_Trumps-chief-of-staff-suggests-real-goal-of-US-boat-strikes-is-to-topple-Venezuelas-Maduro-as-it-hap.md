The US labor market grew by more than expected last month, recovering some of the damage inflicted by the federal government shutdown, according to official data. An estimated 105,000 jobs were lost in October, and 64,000 were added in November, a highly-anticipated report showed today. Jobs growth was higher in November than anticipated by many economists, with a consensus forecast of some 40,000 jobs added. But the headline unemployment rate continued to climb – and hit 4.6%, a four-year high, last month – amid apprehension around the strength of the US economy.
