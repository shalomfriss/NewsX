## Summary

The Kennedy Center added President Donald Trump’s name to the exterior of its building on Friday, just one day after the Center’s Board voted in favor of the change. Critics say only Congress can change the name of the living memorial to former President John F. Kennedy. Several members of the Kennedy family have publicly rebuked the decision.
