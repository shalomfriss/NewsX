## Summary

The U.S. military launched retaliatory strikes on ISIS targets in Syria. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said the attack was a “declaration of vengeance" after two American soldiers and an interpreter were ambushed and killed in Syria last week. NBC’s Julie Tsirkin reports for Saturday TODAY.
