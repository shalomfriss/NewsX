## Summary

A Pakistani court convicted and sentenced imprisoned former&nbsp;Prime Minister Imran Khan&nbsp;and his wife Bushra Bibi on Saturday to 17 years in prison after finding them guilty of retaining and selling state gifts, officials and his party said.
