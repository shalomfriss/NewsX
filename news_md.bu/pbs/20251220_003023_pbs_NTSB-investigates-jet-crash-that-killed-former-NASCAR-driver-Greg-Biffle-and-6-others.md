## Summary

Federal investigators are trying to figure out why a business jet carrying retired NASCAR driver&nbsp;Greg Biffle&nbsp;and six others tried to return to a North Carolina airport just after takeoff but plowed into light poles and a tree line short of the runway, killing everyone on board.
