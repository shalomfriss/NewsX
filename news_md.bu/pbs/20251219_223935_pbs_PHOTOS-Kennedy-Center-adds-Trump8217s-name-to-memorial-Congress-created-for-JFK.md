## Summary

Congress named the performing arts center as a living memorial to Kennedy in 1964, the year after he was assassinated.&nbsp;The law explicitly prohibits&nbsp;the board of trustees from making the center into a memorial to anyone else, and from putting another person's name on the building's exterior.
