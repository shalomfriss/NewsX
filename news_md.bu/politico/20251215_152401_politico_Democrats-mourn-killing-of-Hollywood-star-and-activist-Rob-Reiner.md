# Democrats mourn killing of Hollywood star and activist Rob Reiner

**Author:** By Cheyanne M. Daniels

## Summary

The director often donated to and advocated for progressive causes.

## Content

<p>Democrats are mourning the death of actor and director Rob Reiner and his wife Michele Singer Reiner after they were found dead in their California home on Sunday. The Hollywood star was known not only for classic films like “The Princess Bride” and “When Harry Met Sally,” but for his outspoken support of progressive causes.</p> 
<p>Former President Barack Obama said he and Michelle Obama were “heartbroken” by the news. Former Vice President Kamala Harris said Reiner “fought for America’s democracy.” And former Speaker Nancy Pelosi (D-Calif.) called him “remarkable and excellent” in everything he pursued.</p> 
<p>“Rob’s achievements in film and television gave us some of our most cherished stories on screen,” <a href="https://x.com/barackobama/status/2000442913885712602?s=46" target="_blank">Obama said in a statement</a>. “But beneath all of the stories he produced was a deep belief in the goodness of people — and a lifelong commitment to putting that belief into action. Together, he and his wife lived lives defined by purpose. They will be remembered for the values they championed and the countless people they inspired.”</p> 
<p>Reiner and Singer Reiner’s bodies were discovered in their Los Angeles home on Sunday after the Los Angeles Fire Department responded to a medical aid request shortly after 3:30 p.m., <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2025/12/14/2-people-found-dead-in-home-owned-by-rob-reiner-ap-source-says-00690378" target="_blank">according to The Associated Press</a>.</p> 
<p>Authorities are investigating their deaths as an “apparent homicide,” said Capt. Mike Bland of the Los Angeles Police Department. Authorities announced on Monday that Reiner's son, Nick Reiner, is in custody as a suspect in the case. He has been booked for murder and is being held on $4 million bail.</p> 
<p>Senate Minority Leader Sen. Chuck Schumer <a href="https://x.com/SenSchumer/status/2000564623326495130?s=20" target="_blank">called the news</a> of the Reiners’ death “horrific.”</p> 
<p>“Not only was Rob an incredibly talented actor &amp; director, he was also a relentless defender of democracy and the values so many of us share,” Schumer said. “He will be missed dearly. My prayers this morning are with the Reiner family and all those who loved his movies and what he and Michele stood for.”</p> 
<p>In her own statement, <a href="https://x.com/speakerpelosi/status/2000423280579064242?s=46" target="_blank">Pelosi reflected on Reiner’s contributions</a> to Democratic causes.</p> 
<p>“Personally, Rob cared deeply about people and demonstrated that in his civic activities — whether by supporting the First 5 initiative or fighting against Prop 8 in California,” said Pelosi, referring to the California Children and Families Commission, which supports programs for children under 5 years old. Proposition 8 was California’s 2008 ballot proposal to ban same-sex marriage.</p> 
<p>Pelosi continued, “Civically, he was a champion for the First Amendment and the creative rights of artists. And professionally, he was an iconic figure in film who made us laugh, cry and think with the movies he created.”</p> 
<p>The son of legendary comedian Carl Reiner, Rob Reiner was a strong supporter of LGTBQ+ rights and early childhood education. Reiner often held fundraisers and campaigned for Democratic issues. In 2008, he co-founded the American Foundation for Equal Rights, which challenged California’s ban on same-sex marriage. In 1998, as chair of the campaign for the state’s Proposition 10, which led to the creation of the First 5 initiative, Reiner advocated for funding early childhood development services with a tax on tobacco products.</p> 
<p>He was also a sharp critic of President Donald Trump, previously <a href="https://thehill.com/blogs/in-the-know/in-the-know/422509-rob-reiner-calls-trump-mentally-unstable-he-is-aiding-and/" target="_blank">accusing the president</a> of “treason” and being “<a href="https://variety.com/video/rob-reiner-trump-mentally-unfit/" target="_blank">mentally unfit</a>” to serve in office. In an October interview with MSNBC, now MS NOW, Reiner compared the current political climate under the Trump administration as “beyond McCarthy era-esque.”</p> 
<p>“Make no mistake: We have a year before this country becomes a full-on autocracy and democracy completely leaves us," <a href="https://www.ms.now/top-stories/latest/trump-rob-reiner-jessica-chastain-delay-savant-apple-rcna235963" target="_blank">Reiner said</a> at the time. “I believe the way to stop it is to educate people who may not understand what democracy is. They may not know what the impact of losing it is. We have to explain it, us storytellers have to explain to them what they’re going to wind up with if an autocrat has his way."</p> 
<p><a href="https://x.com/kamalaharris/status/2000442127856103609?s=46" target="_blank">Harris on Monday said</a> she was “devastated” to hear of Reiner and his wife’s deaths.</p> 
<p>“Rob Reiner's work has impacted generations of Americans,” she said. “The characters, dialogue, and visuals he brought to life in film and television are woven throughout our culture. Rob loved our country, cared deeply about the future of our nation, and fought for America's democracy.”</p> 
<p>California Democratic Gov. Gavin Newsom praised Reiner — <a href="https://x.com/cagovernor/status/2000433680645537978?s=46" target="_blank">who he called a “big-hearted genius”</a> — for his empathy.</p> 
<p>“His boundless empathy made his stories timeless, teaching generations how to see goodness and righteousness in others — and encouraging us to dream bigger,” said Newsom in a statement. “That empathy extended well beyond his films. Rob was a passionate advocate for children and for civil rights — from taking on Big Tobacco, fighting for marriage equality, to serving as a powerful voice in early education. He made California a better place through his good works.”</p> 
<p>Newsom added that Reiner will be remembered for his “extraordinary contribution to humanity.”</p> 
<p>Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass called the Reiners’ deaths a “devastating loss” for both the city and the nation.</p> 
<p>“Rob Reiner’s contributions reverberate throughout American culture and society, and he has improved countless lives through his creative work and advocacy fighting for social and economic justice,” <a href="https://x.com/mayorofla/status/2000427445061669125?s=46" target="_blank">Bass said in a post on X</a>.<br /></p>
