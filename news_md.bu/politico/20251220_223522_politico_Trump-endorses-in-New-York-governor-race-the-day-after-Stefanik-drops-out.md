# Trump endorses in New York governor race the day after Stefanik drops out

**Categories:** Politics

## Summary

The president backed Bruce Blakeman, a Long Island county executive.

## Content

<p>President Donald Trump endorsed Nassau County Executive Bruce Blakeman in the New York gubernatorial race, a day after Rep. Elise Stefanik (R-N.Y.) <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2025/12/19/rep-elise-stefanik-set-to-drop-her-bid-for-nys-governor-00700879" target="_blank">ended her bid</a>.</p> 
<p>“Bruce is MAGA all the way, and has been with me from the very beginning,” Trump <a href="https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/115754059136466585" target="_blank">wrote in a post on Truth Social</a> Saturday. “Bruce Blakeman is a FANTASTIC guy, will win the big November Election and, without hesitation, has my Complete and Total Endorsement for Governor of the ONCE GREAT STATE OF NEW YORK (IT CAN BE GREAT AGAIN!). BRUCE BLAKEMAN WILL NEVER LET YOU DOWN!”</p> 
<p>Stefanik was long seen as the favorite in the Republican primary to face off against incumbent Democratic Gov. Kathy Hochul in 2026, months after Trump nudged fellow governor hopeful Rep. Mike Lawler into running for <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2025/05/09/mike-lawler-donald-trump-new-york-00336842" target="_blank">reelection to his competitive House seat</a> by endorsing him to remain in Congress.</p> 
<p>But Blakeman jumping into the race earlier this month complicated things for Stefanik, and Trump had publicly <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2025/12/14/blakeman-stefanik-new-york-republicans-trump-endorsement-00689923?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAc3J0YwZhcHBfaWQMMjU2MjgxMDQwNTU4AAEeNkVJQaI-Il_DwFYeoh5ekBHGaLHQc53aZ_660YrVimvNkzbd_226u-R0ZXE_aem_b9ohPe6bjH00gWYn6JRhqg" target="_blank">praised both Republicans as “fantastic people.”</a></p> 
<p>Hochul still holds a double-digit lead over Blakeman in a potential head-to-head matchup, <a href="https://sri.siena.edu/2025/12/16/hochul-holds-19-point-lead-over-stefanik-leads-blakeman-by-25-points/" target="_blank">according to a Siena Poll of New York</a> released earlier this week.</p> 
<p>Stefanik, a once powerful voice inside Congress, has had a tumultuous year, as the president pulled her nomination to be ambassador to the United Nations over concerns about what her departure would mean for the slim GOP House majority. </p> 
<p>But Trump also declined to endorse the Upstate New York congressmember for governor. In her announcement bowing out of the race, she said she would not run for reelection.</p> 
<p>Blakeman attended a Hanukkah reception at the White House with the president earlier this week.</p> 
<p>In <a href="https://x.com/NassauExec/status/2002503366195491045" target="_blank">a post on X</a> following Trump’s endorsement, Blakeman said he was “grateful and blessed” to have the president’s support.</p>
