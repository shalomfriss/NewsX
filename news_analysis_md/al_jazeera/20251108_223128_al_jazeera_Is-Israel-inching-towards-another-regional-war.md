- UNIFIL (UN peacekeeping force) was tasked with monitoring implementation

**Causal framing**: The summary suggests Israeli attacks are happening "as" Hezbollah disarmament fails, implying causation. However, it omits whether Hezbollah military activities, Iranian weapons transfers, or threats to Israel might be relevant factors.

## 3. Propaganda / Agenda Indicators

The article demonstrates multiple propaganda techniques despite minimal length:

**Loaded question framing**: The headline question presupposes Israeli agency in causing "another regional war," framing Israel as the aggressor without examining Hezbollah's role.

**Selective attribution of agency**: Israel "ramps up attacks" (active voice, aggressive framing) while Hezbollah disarmament merely "fails" (passive voice, no responsibility assigned).

**Omission of opposing context**: No mention of Hezbollah as Iranian proxy, designated terrorist organization, or aggressor in previous conflicts.

**False equivalence**: Implicitly frames Israel and Hezbollah as equivalent actors rather than distinguishing between a democratic state and a terrorist organization.

**Missing context on "another" war**: Reference to "another" regional war without explaining the causes, outcomes, or lessons of previous Israel-Hezbollah conflicts, particularly the 2006 war initiated by Hezbollah's cross-border raid and kidnapping of Israeli soldiers.

**Presuppositional language**: "Disarmament fails" presupposes that disarmament was recently attempted or possible, when Hezbollah has consistently refused to disarm for 18 years.

**Assessment**: 🚨 Strong propaganda indicators

**Propaganda Score**: `8/10`

**Scale interpretation**: Clear propaganda techniques through question framing that presupposes Israeli guilt, selective attribution of agency, and complete omission of Hezbollah's role as Iranian-backed terrorist organization.
